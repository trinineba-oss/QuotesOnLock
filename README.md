# Quotes on Lock

An Android app that keeps a quotation on your lock screen.

Android doesn't let normal apps draw on the lock screen, so the app posts the quote as a silent, ongoing notification with public lock-screen visibility. It rotates on a schedule you choose, and the notification has **Previous**, **Copy** and **Next quote** buttons (Previous steps back through the last 50 quotes shown; Copy puts the quote and author on the clipboard, and the app has a *Copy quote* button too).

Rotation never repeats a quote until it has shown all the enabled ones. There is also one switch to turn off the modern authors who are still in copyright (their lines are short excerpts). Tap an author's name (under the current quote, or in any list) to see all of their quotes, and tap a quote type to browse it; from a list you can search, copy, or put any quote on the lock screen straight away. The home screen shows today's quote with large tiles for Quotes & authors, Browse & search, Subjects, Themes, Text style, Schedule, Favourites, My quotes and About & sources, each opening its own page. Tap the heart beside a quote (on the home screen, or "♡ Favourite" in any list) to save it to Favourites, where you can also choose to show only your favourites on the lock screen. Under Themes you can pick a theme: Classic (your phone's colours), Starry Night (van Gogh's painting behind the app and framing the lock-screen card, with optional slowly moving swirls on Android 13+), Northern lights (optionally moving, with an intensity slider), Old book (an aged paper page), or Butterflies (a soft pink sky with butterflies, optionally flying). The moving backgrounds only animate while they are on screen. Themes also offers a live wallpaper: the lock-screen theme's artwork (optionally moving) with today's quote drawn on it, for the lock and home screen; Android asks you to confirm it with "Set wallpaper". The About & updates page can check GitHub for a newer build and download and install it (Android asks you to confirm, and the first time to allow "Install unknown apps" for the app); it can also check weekly and notify you. Subjects (love, death, time, solitude, nature, art and about twenty more) browse quotes across every author; each quote is tagged automatically from its words, so some are untagged and a few sit under a subject loosely. - Choose the font (six built-in Android families), size and text color of the quote. The notification uses a custom layout for this; Android still draws the background, so colors can clash with your light or dark theme ("System default" always works).
- Rotation every 15 minutes up to daily (WorkManager).
- The quote comes back after a reboot.
- No internet permission, no accounts, no network access.

## Lock screen setup

1. Open the app and turn on **Show quote on lock screen**. Allow notifications when asked.
2. In system **Settings > Notifications > Notifications on lock screen**, choose to show the content, and keep silent notifications enabled.

On Android 14 and later you can swipe an ongoing notification away. It reappears at the next scheduled change, or when you open the app or turn the switch off and on.

## Building

Debug APK:

```sh
./gradlew assembleDebug   # app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
```

Needs JDK 17+ and the Android SDK (API 35). The GitHub Actions workflow builds the APK on every push, uploads it as an artifact, and publishes it to the [`latest` release](../../releases/tag/latest), which is the easiest place to download it on a phone. Builds share one debug key, so a new APK installs over the old one and keeps your quotes and settings.
