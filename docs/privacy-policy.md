# Quotes on Lock: privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

Quotes on Lock is a reading and quiet-moments app: quotes on your lock screen, books and essays to read, and calm tools such as breathing, sounds and guided sessions. It is built to keep everything on your phone.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. It does not send anything you do or write to the developer or to anyone else.

The version on Google Play has **no internet access at all**: Android does not give it permission to connect to the network.

## What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps these on your phone only, in its private storage:

- your settings (theme, schedule, which authors and themes are on);
- the quotes you save as favourites, your collections and your own quotes;
- notes, journal entries and reflections you write;
- what you write in Solace's tools (worries put down for later, your lifeline letter, thought records);
- a short private record of which calm tools you used and whether they helped, shown back to you as "What helps you" (over the last 30 days);
- your reading places and bookmarks.

None of this leaves the phone unless you choose to:

- **Backup:** "Save backup file" writes one file to a place you pick (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive you use). Only you decide where it goes.
- **Sharing:** when you share a quote as text or as a picture, it goes to the app you choose.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show the quote on your lock screen, the optional daily quote and morning affirmation, and the controls while sounds play.
- **Run at startup:** to put the lock-screen quote back after the phone restarts.
- **Foreground service (media playback):** so calm sounds keep playing with the screen off.
- **Vibration:** for the gentle pulse in the breathing exercises.

## Reading aloud

The natural voice used for guided sessions and practices is recorded and built into the app. Other text read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine (chosen in your phone's settings). That engine is provided by your phone's maker or Google and works under its own privacy terms.

## The GitHub version

The version published on GitHub (not the Play version) can check GitHub for a newer version and download it. When it does, your phone connects to GitHub, which sees an ordinary web request (such as your IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement. You can turn this off in the app under Settings › About.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Not a medical service

Solace offers gentle companions for hard moments. It is not a replacement for care from people who know you or from a professional, and it is not an emergency service.
