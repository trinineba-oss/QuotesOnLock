S|Arriving|Let your shoulders drop, and take one slow breath. There is nothing to do here except look. Let your eyes rest wherever they land first.
S|The colours|Now follow the colours. The deep blue of the sky, the white of the clouds, the soft gold of the autumn trees along the water. See how the gold sits between the blue above and the blue below.
S|The reflection|Look at the lower half of the picture. The water is so still that it holds the clouds and the mountains upside down. Let your eyes move slowly from a cloud in the sky to the same cloud in the stream.|.5|.72|1.6

S|The whole|Now let your eyes soften and take in the whole scene at once: sky, peaks, trees and water, all quiet together. Breathe out slowly, and let a little of that stillness stay with you.

V|bigsur|Where the land meets the sea|The Pacific coast at Big Sur, California|Painted for the app from a photograph by Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress, public domain|Leisure in its activity is work. The stillness of the sea stirs in waves.|Rabindranath Tagore
S|Arriving|Take a slow breath, and imagine the sound of the sea far below you. There is nothing you need to do. Just look.
S|The colours|Follow the colours from top to bottom: the pale, hazy sky, the deep blue of the open sea, the white of the surf, and the warm browns and greens of the cliffs.
S|The edge|Look at where the waves meet the shore. A line of white foam curves along the beach and breaks against the rocks. It has been doing this, wave after wave, for thousands of years.|.62|.8|1.8
S|One small thing|Find the small dark rock out at sea, standing alone in the blue. Let your eyes rest on it. The water moves all around it, and it stays.|.86|.56|3.0
S|The whole|Now let your eyes take in the whole coast, the headlands fading one behind another into the haze. Breathe in, and as you breathe out, let your shoulders soften like the far hills.
