## About the translations

Most English translations of Rilke and Kafka are still under copyright, so the quotes marked `(tr. Claude)` are
translations made for this app from the public-domain German text. They aim to be faithful rather than poetic, and
they are not the well-known published versions. If you spot an error or have a better rendering, open an issue.

### Camus, Hesse, Murakami, Frankl, Pessoa, Borges, Cioran, Weil, Neruda, Mary Oliver, Szymborska, Kundera and Sartre (Goodreads)

These authors are still in copyright, so no book texts could be checked. Their quotes were picked from the
most-liked entries on Goodreads' community quote pages for each author (for Murakami, from the English translations by Jay Rubin, Philip Gabriel, Alfred Birnbaum and others; Pessoa mostly from Richard Zenith's *Book of Disquiet*; the translators of the others vary by line), then filtered by hand to lines I could
recognise as really being theirs (poets are limited to short excerpts of one to four lines) (Goodreads quotes are typed in by readers and are often misattributed or
mangled; many of the top "Camus" and "Hesse" quotes there were dropped for that reason). The wording is that of
whichever published English translation the reader copied from, so it can differ slightly from your edition. These
are short excerpts; if you spot a mistake, please open an issue.

### Jung (later writings)

About fifteen Jung lines come from later work still in copyright (*Memories, Dreams, Reflections*, the Collected Works in R. F. C. Hull's translation, his letters). They were taken from the most-liked entries on Goodreads (author page 38285) and kept only where they match passages that can be placed in Jung's writings; the many apocryphal "Jung quotes" online (e.g. "I am not what happened to me") were left out. They are short excerpts; the rest of Jung's entries are cut verbatim from the public-domain texts above.

The *Red Book / Liber Novus* lines (label "Carl Jung, The Red Book") are short excerpts (1–4 sentences) of Sonu Shamdasani's 2009 English edition, which is still in copyright; they were taken from Goodreads' work quotes page (6644707) and hand-filtered to lines that are clearly from that book.

### Einstein

Einstein's own writings are still in copyright, and many "Einstein quotes" online are misattributed. The ones included here are lines with a documented source (his essays, letters and interviews, e.g. *The World As I See It*, the 1929 *Saturday Evening Post* interview, his 1950 letter on the "circle of compassion", his letter to Born), taken from the most-liked Goodreads entries where those match the documented wording and written out from the standard published wording otherwise. ### The Qur'an

Verses are quoted whole and unaltered from Pickthall's 1930 English translation, each labelled with its sura and verse number. Pickthall's English is formal and archaic ("ye", "Thee"); words in parentheses are his own interpolations. I chose verses on patience, justice, mercy, remembrance, hardship and ease, death, and humility; they are given as literature and wisdom in the same spirit as the other scriptures here, not as religious instruction.

Legge's (Tao Teh King, Analects), Müller's and Giles's parentheses mark words the translators supplied; the app drops those marks and otherwise keeps their wording. FitzGerald's *Rubaiyat* is a free English re-creation rather than a literal translation, and Hastie's Rumi poems are free versions made via Rückert's German, so they are looser than the originals.

Legge's Tao Teh King marks words he supplied with parentheses; the app drops those parentheses and otherwise keeps his wording.
